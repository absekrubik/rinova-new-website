Application Timeline

Japan Study Abroad Application Timeline

April - May

Research universities and MEXT scholarship requirements

June - July

Prepare MEXT application, gather required documents

August - September

Submit MEXT scholarship application (deadline early Sep)

October - November

University entrance exam preparation and interviews

December - January

MEXT examination and interviews, university exams

February - March

MEXT and university admission decisions announced

April - May

Apply for student visa (Certificate of Eligibility)

May - June

Visa approval, arrange housing, prepare to travel

June - July

Arrive in Japan, orientation begins

Japan Student Visa & MEXT

Visa Requirements & MEXT Scholarship Program

MEXT scholarships are highly competitive and cover tuition, living expenses, airfare, and insurance for international students.

Valid Passport

Must be valid for entire stay in Japan

Certificate of Eligibility

Issued by Japanese institution or MEXT program

Medical Examination

Physical examination report and vaccination records

Financial Evidence

Proof of funds (not needed for MEXT scholarship holders)

Japanese Language Skills

JLPT N2 level or higher recommended for some programs

MEXT Scholarship Benefits

Full tuition + monthly stipend + airfare + health insurance covered

Work Authorization

International students can work up to 28 hours/week or full-time on holidays

Post-Graduation

Jobs placement assistance and work visa support available
