Canada Study Abroad Application Timeline

July - August

Research universities and programs, prepare documents

September - October

Take IELTS/TOEFL exam, start applications online

November - December

Submit applications and supporting documents

January - March

Receive acceptance letters and financial aid offers

April - May

Accept offer, arrange funding, get LOA (Letter of Acceptance)

June - July

Apply for study permit at IRCC (Immigration, Refugees & Citizenship Canada)

July - August

Receive study permit approval, arrange travel and accommodation

August - September

Arrive in Canada, complete orientation, start studies

Canada Study Permit & PR Path

Study Permit & Permanent Residency Essentials

Canada offers clear pathways for international students to study, work, and eventually become permanent residents.

Valid Passport

Must be valid for your entire stay in Canada

Letter of Acceptance (LOA)

From a Designated Learning Institution (DLI)

Financial Evidence

Proof of funds for tuition, living expenses, and return travel

English/French Test

IELTS, TOEFL, or other accepted language proficiency tests

Medical Exam

Medical examination from IRCC-approved panel physician

Work Permit During Studies

Authorization to work 20 hours/week or full-time during breaks

Post-Graduation Permit

Work permit valid for 3 years after graduation (8-month+ programs)

PR Eligibility

Canadian education counts toward Express Entry points for permanent residency
