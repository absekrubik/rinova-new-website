Application Timeline

UK Study Abroad Application Timeline

July - August

Research universities and programs, prepare documents

September - October

Register for IELTS/TOEFL, start applications via UCAS

November - January

Submit applications, take entrance exams if required

February - March

Interviews (Oxbridge) and admission decisions

April - June

Receive offer letters, confirm place

July - August

Apply for UK Student Visa (Tier 4)

August - September

Visa approval, arrange accommodation, travel to UK

September - October

Arrive at university, orientation and studies begin

UK Student Visa Requirements

UK Tier 4 Student Visa Essentials

The UK Student Visa (Tier 4) is required for international students studying full-time degree programs in the UK.

Valid Passport

Must be valid for the duration of your stay

CAS Letter

Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies from university

Financial Evidence

Proof of funds for tuition and living expenses (28 days bank statement)

English Language Proof

IELTS, TOEFL, or equivalents required

Academic Records

Transcripts, degree certificates, and academic references

Medical Examination

TB test from approved clinic in your country

Visa Fee Payment

UK Visa fee and Immigration Health Surcharge

Post-Study Work Visa

Eligible graduates get 2+ years to work in UK after graduation
