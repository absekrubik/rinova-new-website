Why Study in India?

Most Affordable Option

Lowest tuition fees globally. IIT/NIT bachelor's: ₹3-5 lakhs/year; Master's: ₹4-8 lakhs/year. Living costs incredibly budget-friendly compared to Western countries.

IIT Excellence

Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs) ranked among world's top engineering schools. Strong focus on research, innovation, and industry collaboration.

Tech Hub & Startups

India's thriving tech ecosystem. Bangalore, Hyderabad, Delhi NCR: startup capitals with access to innovation, internships, and networking opportunities.

Cultural Proximity

Perfect for Nepalese students. Similar culture, climate, and lifestyle. Shared borders enable frequent visits home. Natural comfort in a familiar region.

Career Opportunities

Work authorization for internships & placements. Access to India's booming job market in IT, finance, consulting. Build network in fastest-growing economy.

Global Recognition

IIT/DU/JNU degrees recognized worldwide. Strong alumni networks in Silicon Valley, London, UAE, Australia. Excellent foundation for further studies or careers abroad.

India Study Timeline

1

June - August: Research & Preparation

Research universities: IIT, DU, NIT, JNU. Prepare documents: bachelor's transcript, passport, CV, SOP. Register for entrance exams (GATE, CAT) if needed for M.Tech/MBA programs.

2

August - September: University Applications

Apply online to universities. Submit: academic records, entrance exam scores (if applicable), motivation letter, recommendation letters. Meet individual university application deadlines.

3

September - October: Entrance Exams

Take GATE (for M.Tech), CAT (for MBA), or university-specific entrance exams if required. Prepare intensively during this period. Results typically released within 4-6 weeks.

4

November - December: Admission Decisions

Receive admission offers from universities. Scholarship results announced (if applicable). Confirm enrollment and pay admission fees within specified deadline.

5

December - January: Student Visa Application

Apply for Student Visa at Indian embassy/consulate. Required documents: admission letter, financial proof (₹5-10 lakhs), passport, medical certificates, affidavit of non-relocation.

6

January - February: Pre-Arrival Arrangements

Arrange accommodation (on-campus hostels or PGs). Book flights. Apply for foreign student ID. Set up Indian bank account (many universities assist). Purchase health insurance.

7

February - March: Arrival & Registration

Arrive in India. Complete university registration and induction. Register with local police (Foreigner's Regional Registration Office - FRRO). Begin academic programs and explore campus life.

Visa & Documentation Requirements

Valid Passport

International passport valid for minimum 6 months beyond intended stay. Ensure at least 2-3 blank pages for visa stamps and endorsements.

Student Visa (X1 Type)

Main visa category for international students pursuing full-time education in India. Valid for course duration plus 60 days. Work authorization limited to on-campus jobs only.

Admission Letter

Original admission offer from Indian university on official letterhead. Must specify course, duration, fees, and scholarship details. Essential for visa application.

Financial Proof

Bank statements showing ₹5-10 lakhs ($6,000-12,000 USD) for tuition and living expenses. Affidavit from sponsor. Savings certificate or property documents. Less stringent than Western countries.

Academic Transcripts

Certified copies of bachelor's degree and transcripts. Attested by previous university. Apostille certification recommended for official recognition in India.

Medical Certificates

Health examination report from authorized physician. Proof of vaccinations (Hepatitis, Typhoid, etc.). Some universities may require additional medical screening upon arrival.

Police Clearance

Character certificate from home country police. Sworn affidavit of non-relocation from notary. Criminal background check. Recent (within 3 months). Ensures security compliance.

FRRO Registration

Register at Foreigner's Regional Registration Office (FRRO) within 14 days of arrival. Obtain residential permit. Mandatory for all international students staying beyond 180 days.
