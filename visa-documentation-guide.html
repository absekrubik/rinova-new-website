Common Documentation Required
These documents are typically required by all countries

Documents You'll Need

Passport

Valid for at least 6-12 months beyond your intended stay. Scan all pages.

Admission Letter

Official letter from the university confirming your admission.

English Proficiency Test

IELTS, TOEFL, or PTE scores proving English language ability.

Academic Transcripts

Official sealed copies of academic records from all previous institutions.

Financial Documents

Proof of funds to cover tuition and living expenses.

Statement of Purpose

500-750 word essay on your academic and career goals.

Medical Certificate

Health examination, X-ray, and vaccination records.

Police Clearance

Character certificate showing no criminal record.

United States

F-1 Student Visa
Visa Type: F-1 Student Visa for degree-seeking students
Processing Timeline
6-8 weeks
I-20 Processing
2-3 weeks
Visa Interview
1-3 days
Visa Decision
Key Documents
  • I-20 form from university (mandatory)
  • Proof of financial support ($60,000-100,000/year)
  • SEVIS fee receipt ($200)
  • DS-160 form (online)
  • Passport valid for 6+ months

Key Tips

  • Can work on-campus (20 hrs/week), full-time during breaks
  • F-1 OPT allows 12 months (STEM: 36 months) post-grad work
  • Maintain full-time student status (12+ credit hours)

United Kingdom

Student Visa
Visa Type: Student Visa for degree-level study
Processing Timeline
3-6 weeks
CAS Request
3-8 weeks
Visa Decision
Key Documents
  • CAS from university
  • Proof of funds (£30,000+ for 3 years)
  • IELTS 6.5+ or equivalent
  • Tuberculosis screening (if required)
  • Passport valid 6+ months

Key Tips

  • Visa valid from 11 days before course start
  • Can work 20 hrs/week during term, full-time holidays
  • Graduate route: 2-3 years post-study work visa

Canada

Study Permit
Visa Type: Study Permit for DLI-designated institutions
Processing Timeline
4-6 weeks
Standard
2 weeks
Express
Key Documents
  • Acceptance letter from DLI institution
  • Proof of financial support (CAD $20,000-40,000/year)
  • Police clearance certificate
  • Medical exam (if required)
  • Proof of ties to home country

Key Tips

  • Can work 20 hrs/week, full-time during breaks
  • PGWP: 8 months to 3 years post-graduation
  • PGWP can lead to permanent residency

Japan

Student Visa (Ryugaku)
Visa Type: Student Visa requiring Certificate of Eligibility (CoE)
Processing Timeline
1-2 months
CoE Processing
2-3 weeks
Visa Issue
Key Documents
  • Certificate of Eligibility (CoE) from institution
  • Passport valid 12+ months
  • Proof of funds (¥2-3.5 million equivalent)
  • JLPT N2 (master's) or N3-N4 (undergrad)
  • Medical certificate and vaccination proof

Key Tips

  • MEXT scholarship: full coverage + ¥144,000-148,000/month
  • Can work 28 hrs/week or full-time during holidays
  • Visa valid 90 days; convert to residence permit on arrival

South Korea

D-2 Student Visa
Visa Type: D-2 Long-term Residence Visa for students
Processing Timeline
3-4 weeks
University Review
1-2 weeks
Visa Issue
Key Documents
  • Admission letter from Korean university
  • Proof of funds (₩10-20 million)
  • TOPIK level 3 (usually required)
  • Medical examination results
  • Background check certificate

Key Tips

  • KGSP scholarship: 100% tuition + ₩900,000/month living
  • Can work 20 hrs/week during studies
  • D-10 job seeker visa available post-graduation

India

Visa Type: Student Visa (X-1) for degree-level study
Processing Timeline
2-4 weeks
Processing
Key Documents
  • Admission letter from Indian institution
  • Proof of funds (INR 5-10 lakh)
  • Medical certificate and vaccination proof

Key Tips

  • Most affordable option (INR 3-8 lakh annually)
  • IITs and central universities highly subsidized
  • Can work on-campus (20 hrs/week) or internship-based
