F-1 Visa Information

Understanding the F-1 Student Visa

The F-1 visa is the primary visa category for international students pursuing full-time degree programs in the USA.

Valid Passport

Must be valid for at least 6 months beyond intended stay

I-20 Document

Issued by the US institution after admission

Financial Evidence

Proof of funds to cover tuition and living expenses

TOEFL/IELTS Scores

English language proficiency test results

Academic Records

Transcripts, diplomas, and academic certificates

DS-160 Form

US visa application form completed online

Medical Examination

Physical exam from approved medical center

OPT Work Authorization

Up to 12 months (or 24+ for STEM) to work after graduation
