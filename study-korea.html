Why Study in South Korea?

KGSP Scholarship

Government-funded scholarships covering tuition, living expenses, airfare, and health insurance. 100% sponsorship for selected programs.

Tech Innovation Hub

Study in the heartland of tech innovation. Samsung, LG, Hyundai, and startups offer unparalleled industry exposure and internships.

Work Authorization

Part-time work up to 20 hours/week during studies, plus full-time opportunities during holidays. D-10 job seeker visa available post-graduation.

Global Recognition

Korean degrees are highly valued worldwide. Strong alumni networks in tech, finance, and entertainment industries across 150+ countries.

Cultural Experience

Immerse yourself in K-culture, cutting-edge fashion, vibrant nightlife, and warm hospitality. English-friendly cities and excellent infrastructure.

Affordable Living

Lower cost of living compared to US/UK. Accommodation, food, and transport are budget-friendly. KGSP covers living stipend (₩1 million/month).

South Korea Study Timeline

1

September - October: Research & Preparation

Research universities, programs, KGSP eligibility. Prepare documents: bachelor's diploma, transcripts, TOEFL/IELTS, CV, SOP. Register for KGSP if eligible.

2

November - December: KGSP Application

Submit KGSP application through official portal. Fill online form, upload documents, select 5 preferred universities. KGSP covers 100% of tuition and living expenses.

3

January - March: University Applications

Apply directly to universities via online portals. Submit required documents: academic records, language scores, essays, recommendation letters. Follow individual university deadlines.

4

March - April: Admission Decisions

Receive admission offers from universities. KGSP results announced. Confirm your preferred choice. Pay university deposits if required for non-KGSP admissions.

5

April - May: D-2 Visa Application

Receive Certificate of Eligibility (CoE) from university. Apply for D-2 Student Visa at Korean embassy/consulate. Required documents: passport, CoE, admission letter, financial proof.

6

June - July: Pre-Arrival Preparation

Arrange accommodation (on-campus or off-campus). Book flights. Apply for health insurance. Arrange bank account setup in Korea. Attend orientation programs if available online.

7

August - September: Arrival & Registration

Arrive in Korea. Complete university registration and orientation. Register with immigration for residence permit. Enroll in Korean language classes. Start your academic journey.

Visa & Documentation Requirements

Valid Passport

International passport valid for minimum 6 months beyond intended stay. No restrictions on entries to South Korea.

D-2 Student Visa

Main visa for international students. Valid for duration of program. Renewable based on enrollment status. Obtained through Korean embassy/consulate.

Certificate of Eligibility (CoE)

Issued by your university. Required for D-2 visa application. Confirms university admission and student status.

English Language Proficiency

TOEFL (min. 80 iBT) or IELTS (min. 6.5) required for English-taught programs. Some universities may accept alternative tests like TEPS or CEFR levels.

Academic Transcripts

Certified copies of bachelor's or equivalent degree transcripts. Attested by previous university. May require Apostille certification for official recognition.

Financial Proof

Bank statements showing financial capacity (typically ₩20-30 million / $15,000-22,000 USD). KGSP students may have reduced requirements. Sponsor's financial documents if applicable.

Health Insurance

Comprehensive health insurance coverage required. Korean National Health Insurance available to registered international students at low cost (~₩110,000/month).

Police Clearance Certificate

Criminal background check from your home country. Must be recent (typically within 6 months). Submitted with visa application for security verification.

Useful Resources & Links

Study in Korea Official

Government-sponsored portal with comprehensive information on universities, programs, scholarships, and visa processes for international students.

KGSP Scholarship

Korea Government Scholarship Program official website. Apply for full-coverage scholarships including tuition, living expenses, airfare, and insurance.

NIIED (Education Agency)

National Institute of International Education. Official agency administering KGSP and providing guidance on study in Korea programs and requirements.

TOPIK Language Test

Test of Proficiency in Korean. Register for official TOPIK exams to prove Korean language ability (Level 3+ typically required for degree programs in Korean).

QS World Rankings

Latest university rankings, program comparisons, and detailed university profiles. Use to research Korean universities and their global standing.

Korean Embassy

Official embassy website for visa information, application procedures, required documents, and contact details for your nearest Korean diplomatic mission.
